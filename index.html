      How to Play

      1. A spectrum shows up with two opposite ends, for example Hot ↔ Cold. Couples cards also ask a question, such as “After an argument, I need…”.
      2. Only the Psychic looks at the screen while the target is placed:
        • Honest answer: the Psychic drags the needle to where they really are on the spectrum.
        • Random target: the game hides a target, and the Psychic gives a one-word clue, like “Coffee!” for Hot ↔ Cold.
      3. Tap Hide for Guessers and hand the device over.
      4. Guessers drag the needle anywhere on the dial, then tap Lock In Guess.
      5. Show / Hide Target lets the Psychic re-check the target at any point without moving the round forward. Guessers look away!
      6. In Teams mode with steal on, the next team guesses whether the target is Left or Right of the needle for 1 bonus point. No steal on a bull's-eye.
      7. Scoring:
        • 4 points bull's-eye (center)
        • 3 points close
        • 2 points near
        • 0 points miss
      8. Play goes on forever. Tap New Game to change modes, decks or players.
